Based on the video **"BONGKAR ISI PORTFOLIO GABRIEL REY! Exclusive!"** by **Astronacci**, here is the breakdown of 
the visual scenes and camera angle changes across the interview:

---

### Key Visual Transitions & Camera Changes

* **[[00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=0)] - Opening / Intro Pitch**
* **Visual:** Wide setup showing Gico (Astronacci) delivering the introduction alone to the camera, explaining his 
goal to learn about crypto directly from a credible industry insider.


* **[[00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=34)] - Guest Introduction**
* **Visual:** Transition to the studio table setup as Gico welcomes Gabriel Rey (CEO & Founder of Trif Group). Both 
hosts are seated opposite each other at the podcast table.


* **[[00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=46)] - Core Interview Setup (Dynamic Multi-Cam)**
* **Visual:** The podcast uses a multi-camera studio setup that switches dynamically throughout the conversation 
based on who is speaking:
* **Wide Two-Shot:** Shows both Gico and Gabriel Rey at the studio table with podcast microphones.
* **Medium Single Shot (Gabriel Rey):** Focuses on Gabriel Rey speaking about his career history starting from 
2013/2014, IT background, and initial Bitcoin investment at IDR 3 million.
* **Medium Single Shot (Gico / Astronacci):** Cuts to Gico listening, taking notes, and probing about crypto 
fundamentals vs. traditional stocks.




* **[[03:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=188)] - Trif Group Portfolio & Ecosystem Breakdown**
* **Visual:** Medium camera focus on Gabriel Rey detailing the diversification of Trif Group, accompanied by subtle 
graphic text/cards on screen highlighting the ecosystem entities (Trif Exchange, Crypto Wave Media, ISX Bursa, Rata 
Kanan Capital, Jungle Padel, Topgolf, and Anytime Fitness).


* **[[06:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=415)] - Regulatory Discussion Scene**
* **Visual:** Angle switches between a medium-close shot of Gabriel explaining why regulation—not quantum 
computing—is the defining factor for institutional money, and a two-shot highlighting Gico’s reactions.


* **[[16:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=995)] - Portfolio Strategy & DCA Screen Explanation**
* **Visual:** Focused split/tight cut on Gabriel explaining the recommended crypto allocation strategy (70% Bitcoin, 
30% Altcoins) and how dollar-cost averaging (DCA) at support levels outperforms short-term trading.


* **[[23:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=1422)] - Trif Valuation & Corporate Financials**
* **Visual:** Frontal medium shots of Gabriel talking about Trif's internal software IP, security architecture built 
in-house since 2015, and valuation projections (IDR 3.2T to IDR 5–6T).


* **[[30:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=1805)] - "Why Choose Trif?" & Offline Integration**
* **Visual:** Two-shot camera framing showing Gabriel listing competitive features, including native staking features 
and offline top-up integration across 24,000+ Indomaret outlets and OVO auto-debit functionality.


* **[[34:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=2065)] - Deal On-the-Spot Segment**
* **Visual:** Medium-wide two-shot capturing the spontaneous hand-shake/partnership discussion where Gico proposes 
opening an Astronacci-Trif branch live on air.


* **[[38:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_6WEVQ1xWY&t=2312)] - Closing Scene & Outro**
* **Visual:** Wide two-shot of both speakers giving final remarks on regulated crypto growth and US equities, 
concluding with a handshake goodbye and transition to the end-screen graphics.
