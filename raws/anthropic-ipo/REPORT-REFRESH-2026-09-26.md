# REPORT-REFRESH — Anthropic IPO, as of 26 Sep 2026

Supersedes the timing section of `Anthropic-IPO.md` (20 Sep). The core numbers still hold;
**the story moved: the IPO slipped twice and the prospectus is overdue.**

## Executive summary (the new angle)

The biggest IPO in history is **late**. Anthropic confidentially filed its draft S-1 on
**1 Jun 2026**; the public prospectus was expected after Labor Day. As of **26 Sep 2026 the
SEC EDGAR database still contains no public Anthropic filing** (Forkast, 26 Sep). The listing
target has slid **October → mid-October roadshow → November** (WSJ, 18 Sep), with the roadshow
no earlier than mid-October and pricing potentially after the 3 Nov US midterms (Reuters, 4 Sep).
Meanwhile the ask keeps growing: **up to $100B raised at ~$2T** (Reuters, 11 Sep) — still a
reporter/investor number, **not a company-set price**.

## Rules that make the delay legible

- **15-day rule (JOBS Act / SEC):** the public S-1 must be on EDGAR **≥15 days before the
  roadshow**. Public S-1 first → roadshow ≥15 days later → pricing → trading.
- Work backward from a mid-October roadshow: the prospectus **had to land in the first half of
  September** and didn't. Every day of silence pushes the whole chain right.
- Until EDGAR shows it: **no ticker, no share count, no price range, no audited financials.**
  Everything in circulation is unaudited, non-GAAP, or a leak.

## Bull vs Bear (refreshed)

**Bull:** revenue 7× in 8 months and 14× YoY; first profitable quarters reportedly coming;
Nvidia anchoring; $15B revolver + $100B raise = deepest IPO financing ever assembled;
OpenAI benched until 2027; enterprise share leads (Ramp: 43.8% vs 39.8%).

**Bear:** $2T is still a **rumor**, and it prices in perfection; 2025 loss ~$42B;
the compute treadmill ($517B commitments) dwarfs revenue; the prospectus that would prove it
is overdue; rates at 5.44% are killing smaller deals the same week; founders want 50.1% of the
votes with ~2% of the economics; Amodei is asking the industry to *slow down* while he sells
the fastest-growing IPO ever.
