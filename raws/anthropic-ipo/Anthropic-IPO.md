### Bull vs Bear (neutral, both sides)

**Bull (why it could be the biggest ever):**
- Revenue 14× surge in a year ($4.5B → $47B → $65B run rate); enterprise-first strategy gaining share of US business AI spend (Ramp data).
- Massive moat in coding agents (Claude Code/Cowork); "800% a year → 30× revenue = $3T" investor math quoted by FT.
- Raised just under $100B in 2026 alone; valuation already ahead of OpenAI.

**Bear (why skeptics are nervous):**
- $2T is **rumor**, not set — and it prices in perfection; AI sector is "increasingly cautious about spending."
- **$517B compute bill** — a fixed-cost treadmill; capex far outstrips revenue. Top model costs 2.5× OpenAI's flagship (Artificial Analysis) → price-pressure risk as customers cut AI bills.
- Regulation: DoD "supply chain risk" label + litigation; export-control model pull (June 2026).
- The dotcom-bubble comparison is being made openly (Forbes "Is AI entering bubble territory?", TheAIConicle "AI Faces the Dotcom Bubble Ghost").

## "What does this IPO mean?" (the impact angle)

- **For Anthropic:** public capital to fund the $517B compute buildout; loss of the quiet-period secrecy on financials; new quarterly-discipline + governance scrutiny on a **public benefit corporation** with safety-first constraints.
- **For the AI race:** a public-market benchmark. If Anthropic lists at $2T ahead of OpenAI, it sets the valuation anchor for the whole frontier-AI class. OpenAI is the runner-up race (also filed, lower revenue).
- **For markets:** a $2T debut would be the largest ever — bigger than SpaceX ($1.77T) and ~70–80× Aramco's 2019 $29B raise. A giant retail/institutional event.
- **The risk mirror:** SpaceX's own IPO has already fallen ~50% from its peak — the template for what can happen to a mega-AI listing in a few months.
