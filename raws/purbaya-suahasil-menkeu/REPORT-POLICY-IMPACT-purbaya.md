# REPORT: Purbaya Policy Impact — Numbers & Why It Mattered

**Research date:** 15 Sep 2026 | **Scope:** 1-year Purbaya tenure (8 Sep 2025 – 14 Sep 2026) + inherited Prabowo-era macro.
**Method:** old-news deep dive (Sep 2025 – Jun 2026 articles), 14 Sep reshuffle reporting, YouTube Purbaya primary clips.

## TL;DR (for video hook)

Purbaya inherited a **fiscal squeeze** (MBG + Kopdes + weak rupiah) and tried to break out with **expansive fiscal + banking liquidity + aggressive revenue**. His biggest number: **Rp200T Saldo Anggaran Lebih (SAL) into Himbara**. His biggest miss: **rupiah fell to ATH weakness (Rp18.015/USD, 4 Jun 2026)** while two rating agencies cut Indonesia outlook. His biggest win: **growth 5.61% (Q1 2026), credit 7.7% → double-digit target, car sales +12.2% Feb, PMI high, consumer confidence 125**. His biggest culture shock: **"koboi" communication + restitusi pajak + 10 Sep mass reshuffle of hundreds of officials** → internal Kemenkeu blowback → 4 days later, fired.

## 2. Purbaya's signature policies (with numbers)

### 2.1 Rp200T SAL into Himbara banks (Sep 2025)

- **Action:** moved **Rp200 triliun** government surplus funds (SAL) from Bank Indonesia (where it normally sits) to the five state-owned banks (**Himbara**: BRI, BNI, Mandiri, BRI... actually BRI, BNI, Mandiri, + two more).
- **Stated goal:** boost banking liquidity → push **credit growth to double-digit** (from 7.7% → target 10%+).
- **Context:** Purbaya said he responded to BI's Sep 2025 data showing credit only at 7.7% (vs Aug 7.56%) — he wanted to accelerate lending.
- **Aftermath:** created **friction with Bank Indonesia** (monetary vs fiscal policy boundary). In **Jul 2026**, Purbaya disclosed the government **pulled the SAL out of Himbara** after BI "sent a code" to not interfere with monetary policy, then put it back when liquidity didn't behave as promised.
  - *"Ketika BI kasih kode ke saya, jangan ikut campur kebijakan moneter ya saya ikut. Mereka bilang kurangi uang kamu, kami akan ganti"* — Purbaya, Komisi XI DPR hearing, 15 Jul 2026.
- **Market impact:** this is the single most-cited "unusual" Purbaya move (per detikBali 15 Sep analysis).

### 2.2 "Koboi" / data-driven public communication

- Purbaya became famous for blunt, unfiltered statements (e.g., "kalau Purbaya masih senyum, ekonomi aman"), direct confrontation with rating agencies, and public disputes with central bank.
- **Mar 2026:** In sidang kabinet paripurna before Prabowo, Purbaya argued the economy was **"jauh dari morat-marit"** (far from dire), citing:
  - **GDP growth Q2 2026: 5.61%**
  - **Car sales Feb 2026: +12.2% YoY**
  - **PMI: high level (360.7 MSI index)**
  - **Retail sales: up to 6.9 (vs lower prior)**
  - **Consumer confidence: 125.2** (very high level)
- **Jun 2026:** At a press conference, Purbaya dismissed the link between rupiah weakness and fiscal policy, telling critics to "ask BI, not me." The rupiah hit **Rp18.015/USD** that day — the **lowest in years**.
- **Jun 2026:** Purbaya claimed fiscal was fine: **Q1 2026 deficit = 0.93% of GDP (Rp240.1T)**, May 2026 = 0.7%, full-year target 2.68% (Rp689.1T). Primary balance **surplus Rp28T**.
- **Effect:** markets and two rating agencies did not agree → **Moody's cut Indonesia outlook to negative Feb 2026** (on policy uncertainty + governance). Another agency also cut outlook 2026.

### 2.3 Whoosh (KCIC) debt: Purbaya rejected APBN taking over

- **Context:** the high-speed train Whoosh/KCIC (Jakarta-Bandung, CFIJ) had **$7.27B / ~Rp8.37T investment** (per Kompas TV).
- **Purbaya's position:** APBN should **not** service the debt. **Danantara** (state investment vehicle) should handle it — Danantara has **Rp80T/year dividend capacity**.
- **Mensesneg Prasetyo Hadi confirmed** (during ratas) that government is still working out a scheme **without APBN**.
- **Impact:** signaled Purbaya's "don't bail out SOEs with budget money" principle.
- **15 Sep 2026 (day after firing):** Purbaya said Whoosh debt handover to Kemenkeu was on the calendar — tension between Purbaya's stance and political reality.

### 2.4 Danantara dividen Rp120T to APBN

- **Context:** Danantara (the new state investment company under Luhut) paid **Rp120T dividen** to the Ministry of Finance (budget).
- **Purbaya's position:** Danantara has **~Rp80T/year dividend capacity**, so it should be self-sustaining; don't funnel all profits to APBN (per his Whoosh logic).
- **Controversy:** Bhima Yudhistira (CELIOS) said the **Rp120T dividen was protested by Danantara** and was one of the internal frictions that contributed to Purbaya's removal.
- **Why it matters:** it touches on how Prabowo's flagship (MBG + Danantara) is funded.

### 2.5 MBG (Makan Bergizi Gratis) budget cuts

- **Purbaya's move (Sep 2026, Sarasehan 100 Ekonom Indonesia):** MBG budget trajectory:
  - Initially **Rp330T** → efficiency to **Rp260T** → **Rp240T** → Purbaya open to pushing below **Rp200T**.
  - APBN 2027 MBG budget: **Rp232.8T** (BGN 2027 allocation).
  - Purbaya: *"Tahun depan dianggarkan Rp240, tapi saya yakin bisa ditekan ke bawah dengan efisiensi dan pemakaian teknologi."*
- **Why it matters:** MBG is Prabowo's signature program; aggressive budget trim angered program supporters. Purbaya argued for **tech-based efficiency + local Kemenkeu staff monitoring SPPG (dapur MBG)** to prevent corruption/waste.
- **Impact:** tension between "fiscal discipline" and "presidential priority program" — a key political line Purbaya crossed.

### 2.6 Tax reform: restitusi pajak + 0.5% PPh final marketplace

- **Restitusi pajak:** Purbaya's finance ministry **held tax refunds** initially for natural-resources sector scrutiny, but the policy **extended to all sectors** → business community unhappy.
  - Bhima Yudhistira (CELIOS): *"Awalnya restitusi pajak ditahan untuk dicek di sektor sumber daya alam, tapi ternyata di semua sektor. Jadi antara Bimo (Dirjen Pajak) dengan Purbaya beda pendapat."*
- **PPh final 0.5% marketplace (Pajak DHE / e-commerce):** Purbaya's ministry **postponed** the planned **0.5% final income tax on marketplace/digital sellers** (planned to take effect Aug 2026), saying the economy wasn't ready.
- **Effect:** both moves drew **DJP Director Bimo Wijayanto** into open disagreement — one of the internal frictions cited by CELIOS.

### 2.7 Mass staff reshuffle (10 Sep 2026)

- **Action:** Purbaya **reshuffled hundreds of officials** in Kemenkeu (Pimpinan Tinggi Pratama, Administrator, Pengawas, Fungsional, Non-Eselon), mostly in **Ditjen Bea Cukai** and **Ditjen Pajak**.
- **Reaction:** both **Dirjen Bea Cukai (Djaka Budhi Utama)** and **Dirjen Pajak (Bimo Wijayanto)** **refused to attend** the new officials' inauguration.
- **Purbaya's own admission (15 Sep, post-firing):**
  > *"Sebagian ada (karena perombakan pejabat). Sebagian iya"* — acknowledging the reshuffle was a factor in his removal.
  > *"Itu kan hak prerogatif presiden, kita ikuti saja"* — deferring to the president's prerogative.
- **Effect:** 4 days later (14 Sep), Purbaya was fired. This is the single most-cited internal trigger in the CELIOS + media analysis.

### 2.8 Tax reform: AI + National Single Window for customs

- **Action:** Purbaya pushed **AI-powered National Single Window (LNSW)** at Bea Cukai to fight **under-invoicing + transfer pricing** on strategic commodity exports (coal, CPO, ferro alloy).
- **Stated result:** *"Baru saja saya periksa... harga dari sini ke Singapura setengah harga Singapura ke Amerika. Kalau saya sebagai menteri keuangan rugi."*
- **Projected effect:** customs + income tax revenue could **"double or more"** (per Purbaya, 22 May 2026 CNBC clip, 147K views).
- **Why it matters:** this is the most concrete "how it affects the economy" policy — direct revenue uplift from anti-fraud.

## 3. Macro backdrop (why Purbaya got blamed)

| Indicator | Before Purbaya (Aug 2025) | During Purbaya (by 14 Sep 2026) | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rupiah/USD | ~16,000 | **Rp18.015** (4 Jun 2026 ATH low) | **Weakened ~12-14%** |
| Total rupiah loss since Prabowo Oct 2024 | — | **-14.3%** | bearish |
| IHSG (Jakarta Composite) | — | **-26%+** since Prabowo Oct 2024 | bearish |
| Fiscal deficit (Q1 2026) | — | **Rp240.1T (0.93% of GDP)** | within 3% legal |
| BoP (balance of payments) | surplus $7.2M (late 2024) | **deficit $7.8M (2025) / $9.1M (Q1 2026)** | deteriorating |
| Rating outlook | stable | **Moody's → negative (Feb 2026)** + 1 more agency 2026 | downgraded |
| GDP growth | 5.0% (2025) | **5.61% (Q1 2026)** | accelerated |
| Credit growth | 7.56% (Aug 2025) | 7.7% → double-digit target | accelerating |

**Key insight:** Purbaya's **growth side worked** (5.61%, credit, car sales, consumer confidence all improved) but **currency + fiscal credibility side deteriorated** (rupiah ATH, two rating downgrades). This split is why markets felt "wait and see" when he was fired.

## 4. Why these policies "have big impact" — mechanism

1. **Rp200T SAL→Himbara:** direct fiscal stimulus via banking system, bypassing normal monetary policy → **credit acceleration** but **monetary-fiscal friction** → rupiah pressure.
2. **Koboi communication:** raised volatility of market expectations; **two rating downgrades** followed his tenure (not caused by him alone, but associated).
3. **Whoosh rejection:** kept APBN clean but **politically exposed** Prabowo's signature infrastructure.
4. **Danantara Rp120T:** touched the **newest and most opaque state company** (DPI). Friction here = political friction.
5. **MBG trim:** attacked the **presidential priority** → political blowback.
6. **Restitusi pajak + 10 Sep reshuffle:** internal institutional rupture → **Dirjen-level opposition** → the proximate trigger for firing.
7. **AI customs / LNSW:** the **one policy with clear revenue upside** — the "legacy" that survives Purbaya.
