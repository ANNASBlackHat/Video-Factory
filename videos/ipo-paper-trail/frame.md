# Frame — Paper Trail

Bright editorial document autopsy for vertical investigative videos (Bahasa Indonesia). The "anti-dark-finance" look: airy paper rather than neon terminals.

## Canvas
- 1080×1920 vertical, warm paper `#f6f3ec` with deterministic fiber-grain overlays
- Registration crosses corners; persistent page-stack shadow bottom edge; floating paper scraps between shots

## Palette (CSS vars)
- `--paper:#f6f3ec`, `--card:#fffdf7`, `--ink:#141210`
- Highlighter yellow `--yellow:#ffd23f`, marker blue `--blue:#2563eb`, forensic red `--red:#c9402e`
- Manila folder tones `#ecd2a0` / `#dfbe82`

## Type
- Editorial statements: Playfair Display 700/900
- Fine print/documents: EB Garamond 400/700 italic for handwriting voice
- Metadata/receipts: JetBrains Mono; stamps: Archivo Black (uppercase)
- Caption pills: 58px EB Garamond italic on ink-black rounded pill, top:1385px zone

## Components (recurring)
- Polaroid/exhibit cards with tape strips and paper clips; manila dossier folders with tabs
- Hand-drawn SVG ink strokes drawing on (pathLength=1, dashoffset animation); ruled ledger sheets
- Thermal receipt with zigzag clip-path; calendar page with spiral holes
- Magnifier glass drifting over fine print; pen circles (`mix-blend-mode:multiply`)
- Rubber stamps slamming (scale ~3→1); wax seal `.seal` organic blob shape

## Motion language
Physical: pages rain/settle with slight rotation, tags pendulum-swing, wedges tear off pies and fly between documents; softer pace than dark concepts but still avg shot ~2.5s; exits fade exactly at boundaries with visibility kills.
