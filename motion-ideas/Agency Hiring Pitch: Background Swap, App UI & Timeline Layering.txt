**Title:** Agency Hiring Test: Glassmorphic Cards, Glow Rings & Linear 
Slices

---

### 1. Visual Theme & Layout Architecture

* **Brand & Aesthetic:** Sleek modern tech/agency showcase (@render.rush) 
utilizing a faint gray isometric/square grid background, pure white 
floating cards, bright yellow highlight badges, and subtle lime/cyan 
accent lines.
* **Structural Layout:**
* **Before / After Comparison Cards:** Left card holds the "Before" raw 
talking head (smaller scale); right primary card houses the "After" 
sequence with an arched yellow header tag (`"After"`).
* **Static Callout Footer:** The bottom of the canvas features a fixed 
sticky CTA (*"Comment 'Raw' / And I'll send you the raw video"*).



---

### 2. Scene-by-Scene Motion Breakdown

**00:00 - 00:02 | Office Background Swap & Circular Orbit Line**

* **Background Cutout Replacement:** The speaker is rotoscoped out of her 
room background and composited onto a bright, modern corporate office 
background.
* **Vector Accent:** A thin neon-yellow glowing circular vector line 
traces an arc around the speaker (`pathLength` draw from 0% to 100%) while 
floating brand name tags pop below.

**00:03 - 00:05 | Instagram Profile Card Mockup (Full Card Mode)**

* **Visual:** Instagram Dark Mode profile screen (`@personalbrandlaunch`) 
fills the primary right card.
* **Choreography:** Smooth vertical scroll that reveals the follower count 
badge, bio text, and bottom grid tiles with staggered subtle opacity 
fades.

**00:05 - 00:08 | Progress Line Reveal & Staggered Trait Badges**

* **Interactive Line:** A horizontal slider line draws across the 
speaker's chest with a floating hand cursor snapping across the bar.
* **Floating Tag Stacks:**
* Stacked dark translucent pills (`"Talented"`, `"Hardworking"`) enter 
sequentially with yellow square accent icons.
* Followed by 3D emoji bubbles (folded hands / team emojis) springing in 
with slight rotation (`scale: 0 -> 1.15 -> 1.0`, `rotate: -5deg -> 0deg`).



**00:08 - 00:11 | Video Editing Timeline Overlay**

* **UI Asset:** Multi-track NLE timeline layers (color-coded cyan, purple, 
and green tracks representing video/audio layers).
* **Motion:** The tracks expand horizontally from `scaleX: 0` to `1` 
(origin at left) and slide onto the lower third of the speaker.

**00:11 - 00:13 | Animated Keyword Tag Pop**

* **Kinetic Text:** High-contrast yellow headline tag (*"The Editing 
Test"*) springs into view with high damping, locked directly under the 
speaker's hand gesture.

**00:15 - 00:19 | iOS Notification Toast & Vertical Line-Slice 
Transition**

* **Notification Pill (00:15):** Top-center iOS Messages banner 
(*"MESSAGES / Here's some position information"*) drops down from 
`translateY(-30px)` with a smooth ease-out.
* **Vertical Blind Wipe (00:16):** Full-card transition using vertical 
linear slit masks expanding horizontally to reveal B-roll footage.
* **B-Roll UI Bubbles (00:17 - 00:19):** Remote desk work footage paired 
with staggered right-aligned blue chat bubbles (*"This is a remote job"*, 
*"You can expect to work 5-8 hours a day"*).

### 3. Programmatic Motion Specs (For Remotion / Hyperframes)

* **Multi-Track Timeline Growth (Hyperframes / GSAP):**
```javascript
gsap.from(".timeline-track", {
  scaleX: 0,
  transformOrigin: "left center",
  stagger: 0.1,
  duration: 0.6,
  ease: "power3.out"
});

```


* **SVG Circular Path Tracing (Remotion):**
```jsx
const pathProgress = interpolate(frame, [0, 20], [100, 0], {
  extrapolateRight: "clamp",
  easing: Easing.bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1),
});
// strokeDasharray="100" strokeDashoffset={pathProgress}

```


* **Notification Toast Physics:**
* Entry: `translateY: [-40, 0]`, `opacity: [0, 1]`, duration: 8 frames.
* Hold: 40 frames.
* Exit: `translateY: [0, -30]`, `opacity: [1, 0]`, duration: 6 frames.
