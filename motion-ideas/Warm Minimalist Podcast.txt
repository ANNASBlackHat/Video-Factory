**Title:** Warm Minimalist Podcast: Community Radar & Timeline Motion

---

### 1. Visual Theme & Layout Architecture

* **Color Palette:** Warm monochrome cream-to-orange gradient 
(`linear-gradient(180deg, #FDE6D2 0%, #FF8C38 100%)`).
* **Frame Style:** Floating cards with soft drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 
20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`) and heavy corner rounding (`border-radius: 
28px`).
* **Multi-Speaker Swap:** Seamless hard cuts between the two podcast hosts 
while maintaining the consistent card frame container.

---

### 2. Scene-by-Scene Motion Breakdown

**00:00 - 00:04 | Radar / Orbit Network (Full Card Mode)**

* **Header Typography:** Large bold header with italic serif subtext 
(*"Aussie creators / is finding community"*).
* **Radar Disc & Scanning Animation:**
* Central circular avatar (host) pulses gently at the core.
* Thin concentric orbit rings expand outwards (`scale: 0.8 -> 1.0`, 
`opacity: 0 -> 1`).
* A semi-transparent rotating radar sweep line sweeps clockwise (360° 
rotation looped).


* **Avatar Pins:** Memoji/3D avatar pins pop into the orbit tracks one by 
one using a spring bounce (`scale: 0 -> 1.2 -> 1.0`) as the sweep line 
passes over their positions.

**00:05 - 00:08 | 3D Stepped Podium Chart**

* **3D Isometric Columns:**
* Two orange rectangular prisms (labeled "4 years" and "5 years") rise 
sequentially from the base.
* **Choreography:** Linear height growth with a subtle spring overshoot at 
the top. The "5 years" block triggers ~0.3s after the "4 years" block.



**00:08 - 00:11 | Floating Message Bubble & Custom Cursor**

* **Chat Bubble:** Rounded iOS-style chat bubble slides in from the right 
(`translateX(40px) -> 0`, with soft deceleration).
* **Cursor Motion:** A custom vector cursor glides along a smooth bezier 
curve path toward the bottom-right action icon (`+`), simulating an 
interactive tap (`scale: 1 -> 0.85 -> 1`).

**00:12 - 00:14 | Blind Shutter / Line Wipe Transition**

* **Transition Effect:** Speaker 2 introduces a full-card transition using 
a vertical blind slice wipe (vertical linear mask reveal across the card).

**00:14 - 00:16 | Upward Particle Avatars (Dark Mode)**

* **Visual Shift:** Card background flips to deep charcoal black.
* **Choreography:** 3 circular avatar badges launch vertically upward with 
a glowing orange light trail/comet tail, decelerating into place at 
different vertical offsets (staggered depth).

**00:16 - 00:18 | 3D Question Mark Float**

* **Overlay:** Three golden extruded 3D question marks (`? ? ?`) pop above 
the speaker's chest with a floating idle bob (`translateY: ±6px` sine wave 
loop).

**00:19 - 00:23 | Bold Highlight Kinetic Typography**

* **Kinetic Captions:** Word-level color highlights (bold orange font for 
"poppy syndrome", "Australia") synced tightly to speaker cadence.

### 3. Programmatic Motion Specs (For Remotion / Hyperframes)

* **Radar Sweep (GSAP / Remotion):** Continuous rotation: `(frame * 2.5) % 
360` degrees applied to `transform: rotate(...)`.
* **Avatar Spring Profile:** Bouncy overshoot (`stiffness: 180`, `damping: 
12`, `mass: 0.7`) to emphasize the playful emoji/avatar aesthetic.
* **Podium Growth:** Interpolate height directly via `interpolate(frame, 
[start, start + 18], [0, targetHeight], { extrapolateRight: 'clamp', 
easing: Easing.out(Easing.cubic) })`.
