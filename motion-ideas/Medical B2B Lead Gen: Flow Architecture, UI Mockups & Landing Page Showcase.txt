**Title:** Medical B2B Lead Gen: Flow Architecture, UI Mockups & Landing 
Page Showcase

---

### 1. Visual Theme & Layout Architecture

* **Brand & Aesthetic:** Clean health-tech aesthetic with a light clinical 
green palette (`#E8F5E9` background tint, `#2E7D32` dark green text 
highlights, `#4CAF50` active accents).
* **Layout Mechanics:**
* **Card Container:** Vertical white card with curved corners and a soft 
diffused drop shadow floating over a pale backdrop.
* **Speaker-to-Graphic Transition:** Fast alternation between the framed 
speaker and full-card animated schema/mockups.
* **Subject Cutout Overlays:** At specific points (00:37–00:43), the 
presenter is rotoscoped/keyed directly over the UI backdrop so the website 
sits behind her torso.

### 2. Scene-by-Scene Motion Breakdown

**00:01 - 00:05 | Central Clinic Hub & Patient Orbit System**

* **Central Hub:** A clean vector hospital/clinic building enclosed inside

* **Flow Arrows & Nodes:** Four patient avatar badges connected by curved

**00:19 - 00:22 | Floating CTA Pill Over Speaker**

**00:23 - 00:27 | Booking Confirmation Modal Transition**

* **Smartphone In-Hand Mockup:** Re-enters in full-card mode; the Google

**00:30 - 00:35 | Staggered Social Proof Stack**

**00:37 - 00:43 | Website UI Scroll Behind Rotoscoped Speaker**

2. Middle Layer: High-converting landing page UI (Pricing cards: *"Option

**00:47 - 00:50 | Halo Ring Glow & Glitch Outro**

* **Presenter Halo:** A glowing circular green aura expands behind the

### 3. Programmatic Motion Specs (For Remotion / Hyperframes)

* **Circular Orbit / Dashoffset (SVG):**
```css
stroke-dasharray: 400;
stroke-dashoffset: interpolate(frame, [0, 30], [400, 0]);

```


* **Number Counter Ticker (Remotion):**
```javascript
const count = Math.round(
  interpolate(frame, [0, 24], [1642, 2506], {
    extrapolateRight: "clamp",
    easing: Easing.out(Easing.quad),
  })
);

```
