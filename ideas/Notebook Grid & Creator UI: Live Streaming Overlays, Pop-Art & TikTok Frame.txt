### 1. Visual Theme & Layout Architecture

* **Brand & Aesthetic:** Playful creator/graphic studio aesthetic (@psg.graphic) utilizing 
a physical graph/notebook paper background split into light gray/white on top and vivid 
yellow graph grid on the bottom.
* **Structural Layout:**
* **Dual Card Staging:** Left-hand smaller card tagged `"Raw"`, right-hand large elevated 
container tagged `"My Edit"` with hand-drawn vector doodle accents (curved arrow, film 
clapperboards, floating music/CD icons).
* **Branding Bar:** Fixed bottom-right studio signature (`PSG PARSAI GRAPHIC`).


* **Caption Style:** Centered, pill-backed sans-serif captions with bright green keyword 
highlights.

### 2. Scene-by-Scene Motion Breakdown

**00:00 - 00:02 | Mechanical Gear Pop**

* **Kinetic Type:** Pill text snaps into view (*"I think the most / Powerful"*).
* **3D Gear Elements:** Multiple 3D gray metallic gear assets slide and pop into the upper 
right corner of the speaker card with continuous rotation.

**00:02 - 00:04 | Smartphone Cutout & Social Fly-In**

* **Hand Cutout:** Cutout of a hand holding a modern smartphone enters vertically with a 
fast ease-out slide.
* **Social Nodes:** App icons (Instagram, TikTok, X) bounce into position around the 
device connected by dynamic thin line vectors.

**00:05 - 00:07 | TikTok / IG Live UI Simulation**

* **Live Badge:** Top-left red pill badge (`🔴 LIVE  👁 327`) pulses into the corner.
* **Floating Reaction Hearts:** Stream of multi-colored reaction hearts (red, blue 
thumbs-up, white hearts) floating continuously upward along the right edge with horizontal 
sine-wave wobble (`translateX: sin(time) * 12px`, `translateY: float-up`).

**00:09 - 00:13 | Falling Cash & Crowd Silhouette**

* **Falling Dollar Bills:** Photorealistic 3D banknotes fall and tumble across the z-axis 
above the speaker.
* **Dynamic Counter:** Green text badge increments rapidly from `$662` to `$1,000` with 
ease-out physics.
* **Audience Silhouette Card (00:13):** A monochrome vector graphic card showing a 
cheering concert/stadium crowd pops in with the bold label *"Millions"*.

**00:16 - 00:18 | Pop-Art Magazine Card Reveal**

* **Visual Asset:** Colorful comic/pop-art style vintage magazine mockup (*"MAGAZINE / 
Comics 0.99"*).
* **Motion:** Card snaps in with a dynamic spring tilt (`rotate: -6deg -> 0deg`, `scale: 
0.85 -> 1.0`).

**00:19 - 00:23 | TikTok Interface Frame Simulation**

* **UI Shell Overlay:** The presenter is nested directly inside a native TikTok UI layout:
* Bottom navigation dock (*Home, Friends, +, Inbox, Profile*).
* Right-side vertical interaction column (animated avatar with follow plus, like heart, 
comment bubble, bookmark, and share icon).
* Music disc spinning in the bottom-right corner.


* **Kinetic Accent:** Live typing text highlight: `"$0 and it can / go viral"`.

**00:24 - 00:25 | Outro Card Flip**

* **Closing Screen:** Both cards transition to a clean pitch-black card displaying the 
white studio logo (`PSG PARSAI GRAPHIC`).
