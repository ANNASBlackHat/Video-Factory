**Title:** Forest Green Agency Polish: Grid Lines, Cutouts & Currency 
Motion

---

### 1. Visual Theme & Layout Architecture

* **Brand & Aesthetic:** High-end agency portfolio showcase 
(@rovix.studios) using a light textured background, clean white rounded 
cards, and vivid forest green accent typography (`#1B8C3E` / `#22C55E`).
* **Side-by-Side Comparison Layout:** Left side displays the raw/budget 
edit ("5$ Edit"), while the right primary container houses "Our Work" with 
high-framerate kinetic cutouts and motion typography.
* **Typography Blend:** Clean modern sans-serif paired with custom green 
italic serif emphasis words (e.g., *"business tool"*, *"marketing"*).

---

### 2. Scene-by-Scene Motion Breakdown

**00:00 - 00:02 | Technical Grid & 3D Rotating Gear**

* **Background Grid Card:** A full white card emerges with technical 
node-and-line vectors connecting in an asymmetrical modern grid pattern.
* **Central 3D Gear Asset:**
* **Entrance:** Spring scale pop (`scale: 0.8 -> 1.05 -> 1.0`).
* **Idle Motion:** Continuous, smooth mechanical rotation along the Z-axis 
(`transform: rotateZ(...)` ~45° per second).


* **Kinetic Text:** Green bold headline *"business tool"* fades and slides 
up with a subtle letter-spacing snap.

**00:03 - 00:04 | Smartphone Hand Cutout & Click Cursor**

* **Cutout Entrance:** High-resolution cutout of a hand holding a modern 
smartphone slides up from the bottom edge (`translateY(60px) -> 0px`, with 
elastic dampening).
* **Floating Badges:** Instagram and Facebook icon pills hover on thin 
connecting connector lines extending from the device.
* **Cursor Tap:** A stylized yellow pointer arrow glides to the *"Social 
Media"* header and executes a quick scale-down tap animation.

**00:04 - 00:07 | Speaker Card Return & Highlight Captions**

* **Transition:** Smooth cut/cross-fade back to the full talking-head 
speaker card.
* **Text Animation:** Dynamic center-locked captions where the primary 
keyword (*"marketing"*) shifts into bold green cursive/italic serif 
script.

**00:08 - 00:11 | Blank Card Bridge & Punch Re-frame**

* **Visual Breather:** The speaker is briefly replaced by a minimal white 
card carrying transitional kinetic text (*"but also the"*), then cuts back 
to an intensified medium close-up punch zoom on the speaker.

**00:12 - 00:15 | Currency Cutout & Dollar Amount Pop**

* **Layered Cutouts:** Realistic torn USD $100 bill slides horizontally 
across the upper third.
* **Cash Stack & Green Number Counter:**
* Isometric stack of dollar bills slides in from bottom-left.
* Oversized bold green amount (`$1,795`) pops in at the top with a spring 
bounce (`scale: 0.7 -> 1.15 -> 1.0`).



**00:15 - 00:17 | Minimalist Line-by-Line Type Reveal**

* **Closing Typography:** Crisp black lower-third lines reveal 
sequentially per syllable (*"for millions of people / to see your"*) over 
the framed speaker.

### 3. Programmatic Motion Specs (For Remotion / Hyperframes)

* **Continuous Gear Spin:**
```javascript
const rotation = (frame * 1.5) % 360;
// style={{ transform: `rotate(${rotation}deg)` }}

```


* **Cutout Slide-Up Physics:** Fast cubic deceleration curve: 
`Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` across 12–14 frames at 30fps.
* **Interactive Cursor Click Timeline:**
* Frames 0–10: Interpolate `x, y` position along bezier path.
* Frames 11–13: Scale down to `0.85`.
* Frames 14–17: Spring back to `1.0`.
